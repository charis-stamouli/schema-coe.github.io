**Co-PI** &middot; Bren Professor, California Institute of Technology

Aaron D. Ames is the Bren Professor of Mechanical and Civil Engineering and Control and Dynamical Systems at Caltech, where he leads the AMBER Lab. His research develops safety-critical control for robotic systems, with an emphasis on formal guarantees for dynamic and legged locomotion.
