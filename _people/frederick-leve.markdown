**Program Officer, Dynamical Systems and Control Theory** &middot; Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR)

Dr. Frederick Leve manages AFOSR's Dynamical Systems and Control Theory portfolio. He previously served as a research aerospace engineer in AFRL's Space Vehicles Directorate, leading attitude dynamics and controls research, and holds a PhD from the University of Florida.
