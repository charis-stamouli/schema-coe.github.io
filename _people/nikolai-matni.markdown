**Co-PI** &middot; Associate Professor, University of Pennsylvania

Nikolai Matni is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. His research lies at the intersection of learning, optimization, and control, with a focus on the theoretical foundations of learning for control and layered control architectures.
