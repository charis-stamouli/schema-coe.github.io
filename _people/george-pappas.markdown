**Co-PI** &middot; UPS Foundation Professor, University of Pennsylvania

George J. Pappas is the UPS Foundation Professor of Transportation in the Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. His research spans control theory, formal methods, and autonomy, including safety and verification for cyber-physical and learning-enabled systems.
