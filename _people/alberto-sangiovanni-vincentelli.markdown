**Co-PI** &middot; The Edgar L. and Harold H. Buttner Chair, UC Berkeley

Alberto Sangiovanni-Vincentelli is a Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at UC Berkeley. A pioneer of contract-based and platform-based design, his work spans formal methods, design methodologies, and cyber-physical systems.
