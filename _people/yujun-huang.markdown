Yujun Huang is pursuing a PhD in Systems Engineering and Civil and Environmental Engineering (CEE) at MIT. His research focuses on system co-design &mdash; developing mathematical theories and algorithms for the optimal design of complex systems &mdash; directly supporting SCHEMA's compositional co-design of heterogeneous fleets. He received his B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and an M.Sc. in Robotics, Systems and Control from ETH Zurich.
