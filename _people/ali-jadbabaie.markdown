**Co-PI** &middot; JR East Professor of Engineering, MIT

Ali Jadbabaie is the JR East Professor of Engineering at MIT, with appointments in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, LIDS, and IDSS. His research spans network science, optimization, machine learning, and the analysis of large-scale dynamical and multi-agent systems.
