Charis Stamouli is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Automatic Control Laboratory at ETH Zürich. She received her Ph.D. in Electrical and Systems Engineering from the University of Pennsylvania in 2026, under the supervision of George Pappas. Her research lies at the intersection of learning, optimization, and control, with a focus on layered and multitask control architectures.
